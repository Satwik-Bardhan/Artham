Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Artham - Expenses Manager ("the App") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Artham is designed to help you track your personal expenses and manage budgets. To provide these services, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and authentication details securely using Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Financial & Transaction Data: The app stores the transaction details you log (such as expense amounts, categories, dates, and account names) in Google Firebase Realtime Database.
  • App Performance & Analytics Data: We collect crash logs, performance diagnostics, and anonymous usage data via Google Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics to ensure stability and improve the user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data solely to provide and improve the App's core services, including:

  • Synchronizing your expenses and budget data securely across your devices.
  • Authenticating your secure login session.
  • Generating monthly spending charts and PDF financial reports locally on your device.
  • Detecting, preventing, and fixing technical bugs or crashes.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your privacy and data security are our top priorities:

  • All transaction and account data is securely transmitted using encrypted HTTPS/SSL protocols.
  • Your data is hosted on secure Google Cloud Firebase infrastructure.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Deletion and Control

You have full ownership of your data. You may request the deletion of your account and all associated financial logs at any time directly within the App settings, or by contacting us at the email listed below. Upon request, all your data will be permanently purged from our database.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not designed or intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
